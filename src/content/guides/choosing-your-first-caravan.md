## Start With Your Tow Vehicle

Before looking at caravans, understand what your vehicle can legally tow.

The key numbers to find:
1. **Towing capacity** — on the vehicle compliance plate or in the owner's manual
2. **GVM** and your actual loaded vehicle weight — to determine how much towball download you can carry
3. **Maximum TBM** — usually in the owner's manual

## Narrow Down by ATM Class

Most first-time buyers choose too large. Here's a rough guide:

| Tow vehicle type | Practical ATM ceiling |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| Medium SUV (e.g. Prado 150) | 2,500–2,800 kg |
| Large SUV / dual-cab ute | 3,000–3,500 kg |
| Heavy-duty ute (GVM upgraded) | 3,500–4,500 kg |

Leave at least **200 kg buffer** below the vehicle's rated towing capacity. Real-world loads always exceed optimistic estimates.

## Choose Your Layout

| Layout | Best for |
|--------|---------|
| Rear ensuite | Couples, comfort touring |
| Bunk van | Families with children |
| Front bedroom | Full-time or extended travel |
| Toy hauler | Those bringing motorbikes, kayaks, etc. |

## New vs Used

**New:** Warranty, latest features, finance options. Higher ATM for the same price point. May require negotiation on weight.

**Used:** Better value, known defects visible, immediate availability. Watch out for water damage — inspect every seam and corner.

<Callout type="warning">
Always check the caravan's tare weight at a public weighbridge before purchase, and compare it with the compliance plate ATM. Payload claims vary widely between dealers.
</Callout>

## Key Questions to Ask the Dealer

1. What is the **tare weight** as currently equipped?
2. What is the water tank capacity?
3. What is the **full ATM** and is there any remaining payload?
4. Can you provide the **compliance plate** details?
5. Has the van ever had **water ingress repairs**?
