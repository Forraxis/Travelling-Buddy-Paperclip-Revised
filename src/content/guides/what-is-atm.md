## What is ATM?

**Aggregate Trailer Mass (ATM)** is the maximum total mass of a trailer or caravan — including everything loaded inside it — that the trailer is designed to carry.

It is set by the trailer manufacturer and shown on the caravan's compliance plate. It is **not** set by your tow vehicle.

<Callout type="info">
ATM is sometimes confused with GTM (Gross Trailer Mass). The difference: GTM only counts the mass carried on the trailer's own axles, excluding the towball download. ATM = GTM + TBM.
</Callout>

## Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **ATM** | Maximum total loaded weight of the trailer |
| **GTM** | Trailer mass on its own axles (ATM minus towball download) |
| **TBM** | Tow Ball Mass — the download on the tow vehicle's towball |
| **Tare** | Trailer weight with no load (as manufactured) |
| **Payload** | ATM minus tare — how much you can actually load |

## How ATM Relates to Your Tow Vehicle

Your vehicle's **towing capacity** (set by the manufacturer) must be greater than or equal to the caravan's ATM.

Your vehicle's **GVM headroom** must accommodate the caravan's **TBM** (typically 8–12% of ATM).

### Example check

| | |
|---|---|
| Caravan ATM | 3,200 kg |
| Vehicle towing capacity | 3,500 kg ✓ |
| Estimated TBM (10% of ATM) | 320 kg |
| Vehicle GVM headroom needed | ≥ 320 kg |

<Callout type="warning">
Exceeding your caravan's ATM can void the caravan warranty, void your vehicle's insurance, and create serious safety and legal risks. Always weigh your loaded caravan at a public weighbridge before your first trip.
</Callout>

## Common Mistakes

1. **Buying too close to ATM** — most caravanners underestimate their load. Leave at least 150–200 kg of ATM buffer.
2. **Forgetting water tank weight** — a 95-litre tank weighs about 95 kg when full.
3. **Assuming the salesman's weight figures** — manufacturers' stated weights are often for the empty base model, not your optioned-up van.
