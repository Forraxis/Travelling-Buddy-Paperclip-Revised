## GVM upgrades in Tasmania

Tasmania applies the National Code of Practice for GVM upgrades through the Department of State Growth. An approved Vehicle Examiner must certify the upgrade before it is recorded on the vehicle's registration.

<Callout type="warning">
Operating above your registered GVM before formal approval is an offence under the Vehicle and Traffic Act 1999.
</Callout>

## Steps to upgrade

1. Book with a TAS-approved Vehicle Examiner
2. Pre- and post-modification inspections
3. Engineer's certificate issued
4. Registration updated by Department of State Growth
5. Compliance label affixed to vehicle
