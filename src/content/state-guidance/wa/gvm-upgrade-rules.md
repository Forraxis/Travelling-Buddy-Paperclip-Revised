## GVM upgrades in Western Australia

WA's Department of Transport (DoT) processes GVM upgrades via approved Vehicle Examiners following the National Code of Practice. The upgraded GVM is recorded on the vehicle's registration and a compliance label is affixed.

<Callout type="warning">
Operating above your registered GVM before DoT confirmation is illegal and will void your CTP insurance.
</Callout>

## Key steps

1. Book with a WA-approved Vehicle Examiner
2. Pre- and post-inspection of the vehicle
3. Engineer's certificate issued
4. Registration updated with DoT
5. Compliance label fitted
