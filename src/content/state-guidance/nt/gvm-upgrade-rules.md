## GVM upgrades in the Northern Territory

The NT follows the National Code of Practice for GVM upgrades, administered through the Department of Infrastructure, Planning and Logistics (DIPL). An approved Vehicle Examiner certifies the upgrade.

<Callout type="warning">
You must not operate at your upgraded GVM until DIPL has updated your registration record.
</Callout>

## Upgrade steps

1. Engage an NT-approved Vehicle Examiner
2. Vehicle inspected and upgrade assessed
3. Engineer's certificate issued
4. Registration updated by DIPL
5. Compliance label affixed
