## Victoria towing speed limits

In Victoria, standard towing for recreational trailers and caravans is subject to the **posted road speed limit**, not a reduced towing-specific limit. This aligns Victoria with the national approach under the Australian Road Rules.

<Callout type="info">
Victoria does not impose a blanket reduced speed limit for recreational towing. The posted limit applies, subject to any advisory signs and heavy vehicle provisions.
</Callout>

## Speed limit summary

| Road type | Speed limit when towing |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Urban (built-up) | Posted limit (typically 50–60 km/h) |
| Rural roads | Posted limit (typically 100 km/h) |
| Freeways | Posted limit (typically 110 km/h) |

## Heavy vehicle provisions

If the combination (vehicle + trailer) has a GCM over 4.5 tonnes, heavy vehicle speed limits may apply. Check with VicRoads for specifics relevant to your vehicle and trailer combination.

<DisclaimerBox>
Speed regulations may change. Always check current VicRoads guidance and observe all posted signs when towing.
</DisclaimerBox>
