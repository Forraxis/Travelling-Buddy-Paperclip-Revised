## GVM upgrades in Victoria

Victoria processes GVM upgrades through **VicRoads** under the National Code of Practice framework. The process requires a certified vehicle examiner to assess the vehicle and issue an engineer's certificate before VicRoads will update the vehicle's registration.

<Callout type="warning">
Do not operate at your upgraded GVM until VicRoads has updated your registration records. Driving an unregistered modification can result in fines and insurance issues.
</Callout>

## Certification process

1. Engage a VicRoads-approved Vehicle Safety Compliance Certifier (VSCC)
2. Vehicle is inspected and modifications assessed against ADRs
3. Engineer's certificate issued
4. Certificate lodged with VicRoads to update registration
5. New GVM label affixed to vehicle

## Common upgrade amounts

Most light commercial and 4WD vehicles can be upgraded by up to 10–15% of the original GVM, subject to the vehicle's suspension, braking, and tyre rating. Your certifier will advise what is achievable for your specific make and model.
