## NSW towing speed limits at a glance

Towing a caravan or trailer in New South Wales does not automatically impose a lower speed limit than the posted road speed — **unless** the trailer's GTM exceeds 4.5 tonnes, in which case heavy vehicle limits apply.

<Callout type="info">
For most recreational caravans and boat trailers, the standard posted speed limit applies. Always check for advisory signs and road-specific restrictions.
</Callout>

## Speed limits by road type

| Road type | Standard towing limit |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Urban (built-up) | 50–60 km/h (posted limit) |
| Rural / open road | 100 km/h (posted limit) |
| Highway / freeway | 110 km/h (posted limit) |

## When lower limits apply

- **Trailer GTM > 4.5 t:** treated as a heavy combination; 100 km/h maximum
- **Oversize load permits:** may specify lower speeds
- **Advisory speed signs:** always observe these when towing

## Safe towing tips

Even when the legal limit allows higher speeds, driving at a lower speed significantly improves stability when towing. Most caravan manufacturers recommend a maximum of 100 km/h regardless of the posted limit.
