## Overview

In New South Wales, a Gross Vehicle Mass (GVM) upgrade allows you to legally increase the rated weight capacity of your vehicle so it can handle heavier towing loads and payloads without exceeding compliance limits.

<Callout type="warning">
A GVM upgrade must be approved by an authorised engineer and endorsed on the vehicle's registration papers. Operating outside your approved GVM is illegal and will void your insurance.
</Callout>

## How GVM upgrades work in NSW

Transport for NSW recognises GVM upgrades carried out under the **National Code of Practice (NCOP)**. An upgrade is assessed on a vehicle-by-vehicle basis by an approved vehicle examiner and, once approved, is endorsed on your vehicle's compliance plate and registration.

## Key requirements

- The upgrade must be performed by a licensed modifier
- An engineer's certificate (signed by a Level 2 or Level 3 vehicle examiner) is required
- The vehicle must pass a physical inspection before the modified GVM is registered
- Modifications must comply with ADR standards relevant to the vehicle's build date

## After your upgrade

Once approved, the new GVM is stamped on a **compliance label** affixed to the vehicle. Your registration papers are updated to reflect the new limit. Insurance providers should be notified of the change.
