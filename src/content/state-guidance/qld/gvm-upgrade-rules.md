## GVM upgrades in Queensland

Queensland's Transport and Main Roads (TMR) administers GVM upgrades under the National Code of Practice. Upgrades require an approved Vehicle Examiner to certify the modifications before TMR will update the vehicle's registration.

<Callout type="warning">
Your upgraded GVM must be recorded with TMR before you operate at the new limit. Keep your engineer's certificate in the vehicle at all times.
</Callout>

## Process overview

1. Choose a TMR-approved Vehicle Examiner
2. Undergo a pre-modification and post-modification inspection
3. Receive engineer's certificate
4. Lodge with TMR for registration update
5. Compliance label fitted to vehicle

<DisclaimerBox>
This is general guidance only. Confirm current requirements with TMR or a licensed vehicle examiner before proceeding.
</DisclaimerBox>
