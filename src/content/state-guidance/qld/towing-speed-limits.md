## QLD towing speed limits

Queensland follows the Australian Road Rules framework. Recreational towing is subject to the **posted road speed limit**. There is no blanket reduced limit for caravans or trailers under 4.5 tonnes GTM.

| Road type | Speed limit when towing |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Urban | Posted limit (typically 50–60 km/h) |
| Rural | Posted limit (typically 100 km/h) |
| Highway | Posted limit (typically 110 km/h) |

<Callout type="info">
Queensland's higher rural speed limits (110 km/h on some roads) apply when towing, but consult your caravan manufacturer's recommended maximum towing speed.
</Callout>
