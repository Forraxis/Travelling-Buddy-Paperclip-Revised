## GVM upgrades in South Australia

South Australia follows the National Code of Practice for GVM upgrades, administered through the Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT). An approved Vehicle Safety Compliance Certifier must certify the upgrade.

<Callout type="warning">
Do not operate at your new GVM until the registration record is updated by SA DIT.
</Callout>

## Process

1. Engage an SA-approved certifier
2. Vehicle inspection and modification assessment
3. Engineer's certificate issued
4. Lodge with DIT for registration update
5. Compliance label affixed
