## GVM upgrades in the ACT

The ACT administers GVM upgrades through Access Canberra under the National Code of Practice. A registered Vehicle Examiner must certify modifications before Access Canberra will update your vehicle's registration.

<Callout type="warning">
Operating at an unapproved GVM in the ACT is an offence. Always confirm your registration is updated before carrying heavier loads.
</Callout>

## Steps to upgrade

1. Engage an ACT-registered Vehicle Examiner
2. Vehicle undergoes pre- and post-modification inspection
3. Engineer's certificate issued
4. Certificate lodged with Access Canberra
5. Compliance label affixed to vehicle
